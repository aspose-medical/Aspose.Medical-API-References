## ICEchoExchangeHandlerFactory interface

A factory that creates handlers that observe C-ECHO exchanges on the SCU side. Invoked after the remote SCP returns a C-ECHO-RSP, allowing the implementer to inspect the verification status (e.g. success or failure) of a DICOM association connectivity check.

```csharp
public interface ICEchoExchangeHandlerFactory : 
    INotificationHandlerFactory<UnaryExchangeNotification<CEchoRequest, CEchoResponse>>
```

### Remarks

C-ECHO is the DICOM "ping" — it verifies that the remote SCP is reachable and capable of responding on the established association. See DICOM PS 3.7 §9.1.5.
