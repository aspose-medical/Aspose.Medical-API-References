## DicomNetworkConnectionOptions.ReceiveTimeout property

Maximum time to wait for inbound transport activity during a single asynchronous receive operation.

Applies to transport reads rather than to association-level protocol timers.

Default is InfiniteTimeSpan, which disables the transport-level receive timeout.
